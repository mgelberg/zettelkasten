## Trick for resonance
- Mult the output of the [[low pass gate]] and send it back in a feedback loop to the original sound source (Maths channels 2 and 3 is a good option). Invert that second signal. If you want to make the resonance even more pronounced, amplify it before sending to maths (I used veils but in the video they used an actual preamp). Just a way of changing things up.

What I've seen overall is just having multiple envelopes into one channel offers some nice ways of enhancing the sound.
