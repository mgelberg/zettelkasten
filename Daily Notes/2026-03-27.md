Early morning, but Ezra appears to have gone back into his own room to entertain himself. I'll take the time. Had my interview with TouchTunes yesterday. I feel like it went really well, minus their 'practical scenario' question. It felt like it came up way late in the interview, I feel like I gave a bit of word salad. Otherwise, I think the interview went well. Guess we'll have to see. It's not like it's a total black & white scenario between Masterworks and TouchTunes.

Audrey dispelled the notion that somehow one was the more righteous path while the other was the sell out. They both have their pros and cons, one pays more, the other doesn't. If I had to guess, Masterworks would be slightly less stressful, but that doesn't necessarily mean it'd be a more enjoyable job on a day to day. She made the point as well, rightfully so, of how disappointed I was with the layoffs. How it confirmed that the direction I was hoping the label would go in, and the people who were advocating for that change, was not in the cards anymore. At least for the foreseeable future.

All that is to say, if things do progress with TouchTunes, it feels like ultimately the $ thing is going to win out. I'd have to focus my energy outside of work on the creator side of music, which isn't a bad thing. It'd give me a reason to hold onto Metronome (assuming this tax return doesn't completely fuck me). Masterworks would have to come up with a fairly compelling argument to keep me on. I don't feel like I'd be totally blindsiding them either, I've made very clear to Zach that I needed to do my own "due diligence," and have repeated requested that out clause.

Again, I don't want to get so ahead of myself, but it's just an interesting time. I feel grateful that I even had an interview.
